03 · WHY THERE

Three reasons the parts come from where they come from.

CARRIED OVER FROM CARS

The same firms, one industry earlier

Top Group, Sanhua, Wuzhou New Spring and Double Ring were already inside Tesla's automotive supply chain. A decade of precision manufacturing, cost control and fast iteration in electric vehicles carries across to joints.

ONE-HOUR CIRCLE

A cluster, not a set of vendors

Zhejiang, Jiangsu and Shanghai hold a supply base built around Tesla's car programmes, dense enough that a sample can move in twenty-four hours. That density is what Mexico and Berlin cannot stand up quickly.

PRICE

The $20,000 target needs it

Green Harmonic's harmonic reducers are reported at 40–60% of the equivalent from Japan's Harmonic Drive, and Wuzhou New Spring's roller screws at about half of Switzerland's Rollvis. Without that, the BOM target is out of reach.

04 · WHAT WOULD BREAK IT

Three risks worth pricing before the orders land.

SCHEDULE

50,000 to 150,000 is a wide band

The 2026 guidance spans a factor of three. At the lower bound the operating leverage across the whole chain roughly halves.

CONCENTRATION

One customer, most of the revenue

Tesla is already close to 40% of Top Group's revenue, and humanoid work is a fast-rising share at Green Harmonic. A slower order pace compresses both.

LOCALISATION

North America builds its own

Once Tesla's Mexico plant is fully up, Rollvis and Harmonic Drive's US plant compete for the same content. Top Group and Green Harmonic have both planned Mexican joint ventures against that.
