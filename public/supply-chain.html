03 · WHY THIS SUPPLY BASE

Three reasons the parts come from where they come from.

CARRIED OVER FROM CARS

The same firms, one industry earlier

Tuopu, Sanhua and Shuanghuan were inside Tesla's automotive supply chain first. A decade of precision manufacturing and cost control in electric vehicles carries across to joints.

ONE-HOUR CIRCLE

A cluster, not a list of vendors

Zhejiang, Jiangsu and Shanghai hold a base dense enough to move a sample in twenty-four hours. That density is what a new site cannot stand up quickly.

PRICE, PART BY PART

The target does not close without it

Harmonic reducers at 40–60% of the Japanese price, roller screws at about half the Swiss one. Without this base, one estimate puts the same robot at 2.8 times the cost.

04 · EDEN FACTORY · U.S. OPEN LINE · IN BUILD

We are building the first open, compliant robot production line in the U.S.

After FCC DA 26-786 put foreign-produced advanced robots on the Covered List, where a robot is built stopped being a cost decision and became a market-access one. Eden is a shared, open final-assembly line in North America: core components sourced to Chinese T1 spec through non-Covered paths, assembled here, with the origin of every part on record.

500+factories and supply-chain partners behind the line
60+T1 suppliers across 11 robotics categories

100%BOM traceability — origin recorded for every part
