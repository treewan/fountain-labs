02 · REFERENCE · WHAT A HUMANOID NEEDS

What the parts are for, and how many of each go in.

Tesla is the only programme to have published a bill of materials in any detail, so its numbers stand in as the reference shape of the problem. Read them as scale, not gospel.

The BOM target is about $20,000 a unit at mass production, down from roughly $43,000 in small batches. Guidance has been 50,000–150,000 units in 2026.

Around 70% of that chain is reported as Chinese, for a specific reason rather than a general one: harmonic reducers at 40–60% of the Japanese equivalent, planetary roller screws at about half the Swiss one. The $20,000 target does not close without it.

What would unwind it: the schedule spans a factor of three, several suppliers now carry Tesla at close to 40% of revenue, and once the Mexico plant is fully up, local makers compete for the same content.
