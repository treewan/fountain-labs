03 · FOUR VERTICALS

Where we invest, and what we know how to build.

01

AI Hardware & Wearables — Consumer & Prosumer

AI-native consumer and pro-grade devices spanning hardware, context, models and interaction: smart glasses, rings, earbuds, desktop companions, Agent Computers, cameras, portable terminals — from sensor miniaturisation and on-device models to new interaction paradigms.

02

Robotics & Models

Robot-first teams — service, manipulation and mobile platforms, dexterous hands included — scaling from prototype to small-batch production. Model-first teams (VLA, world models) building their own embodiments to close the data loop and validate intelligence in the physical world.

03

Physical AI Infra

Data engines, simulation and teleoperation, sensing and evaluation tooling. Real-world data is the anchor sector: acquisition and annotation → world-model training → embodied deployment, closing the loop from data to models to behaviour.

04

American Dynamism

AI-native American brands for the reindustrialisation era — AI products serving lifestyle and vertical industries, and AI-powered solutions that raise the localisation of American manufacturing, pairing Bay Area product definition with world-class execution.
