01 · WHY A U.S. OPEN LINE

After FCC DA 26-786, where and by whom a robot is built is a regulatory question. We turn it into a production line.

The FCC placed foreign-produced advanced robots on the Covered List. For U.S. labs and robotics companies, a Shenzhen-assembled body is no longer a cost decision; it is a market-access decision.

Eden Factory is a shared final-assembly line in North America. Core components are sourced to Chinese T1 specifications through non-Covered supply paths in Southeast Asia, Japan, Korea, Taiwan and the U.S., then assembled here with full BOM traceability. Customers get compliant capacity without building a factory.
