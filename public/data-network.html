01 · WHO IT IS FOR

Four kinds of models, one source of real work.

WORLD MODELS
Continuous video with physical cause and effect

Unedited, real-time footage where the subject of every frame is an action changing the state of an object. Scene diversity the open web does not have.

EMBODIED AI
Human manipulation at working distance

Arm's-length, near-first-person views of hands and tools, plus targeted first-person collection to your task list. Pre-training scale without teleoperation cost.

SPATIAL AI
Spaces in progress, not finished spaces

Walk-through video of sites under construction, renovation and inspection. The in-between states that synthetic and scanned datasets leave out.

TEMPORAL
The same place, day after day

Workers photograph the same spot every shift. Sequenced by timestamp and GPS, a building grows from foundation to roof: before/after pairs at zero labeling cost.

Our own Ego terminal and data glove turn footage into action-labeled data.

Lab-grade first-person capture needs trackers and instrumented gloves that never leave the lab. We build the same capability as low-cost wearables workers can put on for a shift, and distribute them through a network that already exists.

08 · WHY IT EXISTS

Every hour of Physical AI data has to be captured by someone.

Language models trained on an internet that already existed. Physical AI has no such archive. Lab teleoperation covers a few hundred operators and a few scenes; the long tail of real work is not in it.

Timemark is the camera workers already use to document their jobs. We built the network that turns that footage into training data, and the Singapore pipeline that delivers it.
