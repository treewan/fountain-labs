01 · THE PROBLEM

Model companies are being dragged into hardware they never wanted to own.

The stack has split into brains and bodies. The brain companies run on other people's hardware, and their options are whole robots they re-characterize by hand, or bare components with no firmware, no measurements and no safety chain.

Autos solved this decades ago with the Tier-1: a supplier who designs the deterministic hardware under the software stack, stands behind it, and never touches the stack. Nora is that Tier-1 for the motion domain of robots.

02 · THE PRODUCT

Three parts, one contract.

MODULES
Joint modules that are the same joint, every unit, every revision

Motor, reducer, encoder and drive with Nora firmware: FOC and torque loops, safe torque off in the drive, a per-unit conformance record that ships with every module. Shenzhen and Suzhou supply, U.S. counterparty.

CONTROLLER
The box that runs your low-level policy deterministically

1 kHz executor with verified timing bounds, EtherCAT master, IMU and F/T with hardware timestamps, and an independent safety MCU carrying the functions a learned policy cannot.

MEASUREMENTS
Your body inside your training distribution

Per-joint dynamics measured on a rig, exported as Isaac Lab / MuJoCo assets validated against real trajectories. Deployed fleets re-identify themselves and flag drift.

CONTRACT V1 · THE LINE NORA NEVER CROSSES
Your policy sends joint targets and gains at 50 Hz. Nora returns state at 1 kHz. Everything above the line is yours; everything below is delivered characterized, versioned and safety-floored.
